Life story
Her family was Albanian. At the age of twelve, she heard the calls of God. Knowing that she had to be a missionary to spread the love of God. At eighteen, she left her home in Skopje and Joined Sisters of Loreto, an Irish community of nuns in India.
How did she become a saint?
The Vatican ascribes sainthood to whom says have proven to perform miracles after death. Teresa died in 1997, age 87, and according to the church cured an Indian woman of a stomach tumor, and a Brazilian man of a brain infection.
